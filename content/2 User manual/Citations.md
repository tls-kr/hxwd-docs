An information node consisting of a [[Syntactic Word]], a text line and, possibly, a translation will be called a citation.  The citation is a result of an attribution added to the system from [[The attribution floater|the attribution floater]]. These are sometimes called **attributions** or **SWL** (Syntactic Word Location)

The citations can form building blocks for analysis of the texts and our understanding.  The existing citations can be browsed and inspected from various perspectives.
