An information node consisting of a [[Syntactical Word]], a text line and, possibly, a translation will be called a citation.  The citation is a result of an attribution added to the system from [[The attribution floater|the attribution floater]]. 

The citations can form building blocks for analysis of the texts and our understanding.  The existing citations can be browsed and inspected from various perspectives.
