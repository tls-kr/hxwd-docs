The HXWD website implements methods to support the research process, based on the principles laid out in the [[1 General introduction/0verview|Introduction]].

The 

In an influential paper published in 2000, John Unsworth made suggestions on what consists 'scholarly primitives' common to research activities in all fields of the Humanities[1]
- **Discovering**
- **Annotating**
- **Comparing**
- **Referring**
- **Sampling**
- **Illustrating**
- **Representing**

1  [Scholarly Primitives: what methods do humanities researchers have in common, and how might our tools reflect this?](https://people.brandeis.edu/~unsworth/Kings.5-00/primitives.html) (King's College, 2000)
