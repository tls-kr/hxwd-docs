The project also includes a commented bibliography with currently more than 1600 titles. This bibliography is constantly updated and referenced from relevant locations in the database, providing further evidence or discussion for claims made. 

Access to the bibliography is available by selecting 'Bibliography' from the 'Browse' menu available on every page, or by using the search function in the top right part of the page, with the search target selected as 'bibliography', as shown below.
