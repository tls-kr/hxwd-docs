In the context of the TLS, a Concept is a label for a set of [[Words]] that have a role in the semantic field which they together define.  A Word here is a subset of all the meanings a [[Charaters|Character]] can have, that is, the one meaning that is actualized by being grouped together with the other Words under a given label.  Sometimes there is a definition for the Word as such, but in most cases, a specific meaning is given for each of the [[Syntactic Word]] that are enumerated for a given word. 

In addition to the way this group of Words contribute together to the semantic field of the Concept, the Concept derives its semantics from a definition and by its place in a [[Taxonomy of Concepts]].
