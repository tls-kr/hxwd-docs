Rhetorical devices are used in a very broad sense to describe features of a text above the syntactical level, which includes all manners of style, narrative strategy or way of expression of a text.

There are currently more than 600 items defined, which are arranged in a hierarchical classification tree. The names follow the conventions of European rhetoric analysis, which uses Latin labels to name the device, followed by a translation into modern Chinese.
